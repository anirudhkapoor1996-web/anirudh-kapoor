00The Premise

A practice organised around one conviction: that objective logic and subjective taste are not opposites, but instruments of the same intelligence.

Anirudh Kapoor works across interiors, construction, product, signage, furniture, artwork consultancy, real estate, branding, automotive writing, AI systems, linguistics, and a podcast. Today that breadth is invisible — scattered across a Wix template, iCloud, Drive, and hard drives. This document defines a single home for it: part museum, part archive, part laboratory. Every section that follows is built, not described.

01
The Audit

Where the work lives today, and why that is the problem.

The current site — anirudh-kapoor.com, built on Wix — presents three project categories (Product, Graphic, Interiors) and a booking link. It is competent and empty. It reads as a freelancer's template, not the record of a polymath. The breadth that is the entire point of your practice is the one thing a visitor cannot see.

Meanwhile the actual work sits in folders nobody will ever open. The asset and the audience never meet. That is the gap this blueprint closes.

11
Testimonials · Reconsidered

The Witness.

Fabricated client quotes are beneath this brand. But your instinct is the better one: rather than pretend, let an AI testify openly — read the entire body of work and state, on the record and clearly labelled, what it observes through a non-human eye. Honest by construction, and uniquely fitting for someone who works in AI. One assessment, with full provenance, says more than a wall of praise.

The Witness sits alongside the human record, never instead of it:

References
Real collaborators, named on request. Quality over quantity.
Recognition
Press, features, certificates, talks — verifiable third-party proof.
Why this is ethical

It never impersonates a human client. The source, method, and prompt are published. The reader knows exactly what they are reading — a transparent, repeatable machine reading of the work itself.
