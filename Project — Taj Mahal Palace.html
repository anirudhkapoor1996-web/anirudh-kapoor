The Taj Mahal Palace

A festive-gifting suite for The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai — four complete hamper routes, a set of Mughal-jali and baroque wrapping papers, and outer-box charms. The brief was one idea expressed four ways: the box, the paper and the ornament as a single, holistic unboxing.

Property
The Taj Mahal Palace · Mumbai
Scope
Festive hampers & gifting
Year
2018
System
Boxes · wrapping · charms
Role
Packaging design & art direction
01Overview
The box is the first gift.

At a property like The Taj Mahal Palace, a festive hamper is a piece of the brand that leaves the building — so the packaging has to carry the same weight as the room service and the lobby. The brief was a gifting system built as a single experience: a hero box, a wrapping paper and a set of ornaments that together make the opening feel like an event.

Rather than settle on one look, the work offers four complete routes, each with its own colour, material and reference, so the property can match the hamper to the occasion.

02Four Routes
03

One Equation

Box plus paper plus ornament equals a moment.

The system is set out as a simple equation — a hero box, a wrapping paper and a set of complementary ornaments combining into a single holistic unboxing. Each route keeps that structure; only the materials and motifs change. It's a way of giving the property variety without ever losing the thread of one designed experience.

04

Four Worlds

Route one is cobalt leather, festive and modern. Route two takes paisley as a homage to Persian language and craft. Route three is a contemporary chrome box with a laser-etched jali, all reflection and shadow. Route four is colonial green velvet with gold-tag embossing — old-school luxury. Each carries its own mood board and palette, and each lines its box in a different texture.

05

Paper & Ornament

Around the boxes sit the wrapping papers — Mughal-jali and baroque motifs drawn in the Taj's golds — and a kit of outer-box charms: tassels, rosettes and metal medallions. The small things are where festive gifting earns its premium, so they were treated as part of the design, not an afterthought.
