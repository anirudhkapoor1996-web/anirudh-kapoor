Mystic Mountain — Jageshwar

A tri-fold brochure for Mystic Mountain, a Himalayan retreat at Jageshwar in Uttarakhand. Forest-green panels, misty mountain photography and a calm editorial grid carry the property, its accommodation, and its programs.

Client
Mystic Mountain · Jageshwar
Scope
Tri-fold brochure
Year
2019
Sections
Property · stay · Explore / Experience / Rejuvenate
Role
Editorial design & layout
01Overview
Sell the silence, not the room.

Mystic Mountain is a retreat in Jageshwar — deodar forest, old temples, thin mountain air. The brochure had to sell that stillness in a piece small enough to slip into a pocket, so it leans on space, deep forest green and a few well-chosen photographs rather than a hard sell.

Folded as an A5 tri-fold, it opens from a misty cover into the property, the rooms, and three ways to spend the days there.

02The Brochure
03

Green & Mist

A palette taken from the place.

The whole piece sits on a single deep forest green, with photography doing the talking — a lone figure on a misty road, deodar canopies, the carved Jageshwar temples, the property terraced into the hillside. Type stays light and well-leaded, so each panel breathes the way the location does.

04

Explore, Experience, Rejuvenate

The inner spread organises the stay into three clear promises — Explore (treks and the temples), Experience (local life and adventure), Rejuvenate (the slow, restorative side) — each anchored by a small photo grid. The fold sequence is designed so the reader uncovers the place the way a guest would arrive at it.
