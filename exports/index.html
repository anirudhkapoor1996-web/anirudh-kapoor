04 — How it works

From inquiry to your warehouse, handled.

01

Inquiry & brief

You tell us the products, volumes, target price, and timeline. No account or commitment needed.

02

Curated proposal & quote

We return a curated edit with maker options and a written quotation — pricing shared privately against your brief.

03

Samples & approval

Physical or photographic samples for sign-off before any bulk commitment.

04

Production & QC

We manage the makers, timelines, and a documented quality check before despatch.

05

Export, docs & freight

Packing, export documentation, and logistics to your port or door — Incoterms to suit.

06

Delivery & reorder

Delivered, with a clean reorder path and a kept record of your specs for next time.

Wholesale pricing is shared privately, against your brief and volumes — never published.
