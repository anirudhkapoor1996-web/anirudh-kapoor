01The idea

Never too few recipes. Too many.

Every evening, the same negotiation. The internet answers it with ten thousand options, endlessly scrollable, none of them actually chosen.

Khao does the opposite. One dish. Wrong? Swipe. Right? Cook it. It doesn't decide for you — it clears the field until deciding is possible again.

02What it does
Never bends

Diet rules are absolutes

Jain, vegan, vegetarian, egg, allergies, a fasting day. Checked before anything is suggested — not weighed against other preferences.

Everyone at once

Built for a household

One person eats meat, another never has. Khao holds every person's rules together and offers only what works for all of them.

Stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up

It runs on the device. Nothing to create, and your dietary and religious choices are not sent to a server.

English and Hindi

Knows the calendar

Festivals and vrat days

Restricted days are understood rather than ignored, so what you're offered is something you can actually eat.
