01What it is

The problem was never too few recipes. It was too many.

Every evening, the same negotiation: what should we eat? The internet answers with ten thousand recipes, endlessly scrollable, none of them actually chosen.

Khao does the opposite. It shows you one dish. If it's wrong, swipe it away. If it's right, cook it. It doesn't decide for you — it clears away enough of the field that deciding becomes possible again.

02What it does
Never bends

Diet rules are absolutes

Jain, vegan, vegetarian, egg, allergies, a fasting day. Khao treats these as hard rules checked before anything is suggested — not as preferences to be traded off.

Everyone at once

Built for a household

One person eats meat, another never has. Khao holds every person's rules together and only offers what works for all of them.

Stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up

Khao works on the device. There is no account to create, and your dietary and religious choices are not sent to a server.

दोनों भाषाएँ

English and Hindi

Fully bilingual, switchable whenever you like — including the dish names, not just the buttons.

Knows the calendar

Festivals and vrat days

Restricted days are understood rather than ignored, so what Khao offers on those days is something you can actually eat.
