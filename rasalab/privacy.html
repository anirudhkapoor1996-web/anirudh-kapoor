Privacy Policy

Last updated — July 2026

AK.RasaLabs (youtube.com/@ak.rasalabs) is an independent, single-person music project operated by Anirudh Kapoor. This page explains, plainly, what happens to information connected to this site and its channels.

What this site collects

This page (anirudh-kapoor.com/rasalab) does not use cookies, does not run analytics, and does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors. It is a static page — there are no forms, no accounts, and no tracking scripts.

YouTube and Instagram

Links on this page lead to AK.RasaLabs's YouTube channel and Instagram profile. Once you follow one of those links, your interaction is governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms — not this page:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy

Publishing

AK.RasaLabs uses the YouTube Data API solely to publish AK.RasaLabs's own original content to AK.RasaLabs's own YouTube channel. This does not collect data from viewers, does not access viewer accounts, and does not read or store data belonging to anyone other than the channel owner.
