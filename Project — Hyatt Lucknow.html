Hyatt Regency Lucknow

Poster and promo design across the property's outlets — the UPS bar, Rocca, Lukhin and The Market. Many tables, one design voice: a black-and-gold bar promo, a sunlit mango campaign, a carvery, a winter tea.

01Overview
One property, several restaurants, one hand.

A hotel runs many kitchens, and each needs to sell itself without the others losing the thread. This is a run of promo posters for the outlets of the Hyatt Regency, Lucknow — a bar, an Italian grill, a Lucknowi carvery and an all-day deli — held together by a consistent approach to type, image and offer.

The moods change with the room: graphic and nocturnal for the bar, warm and photographic for the deli, seasonal for a mango month — but the design hand stays the same.

02The Campaigns
03

Range

A bar at midnight; a deli at breakfast.

"Double Drinks" goes graphic — two tumblers drawn in line on near-black, the offer in gold. "Majestic Mango" does the opposite, a glossy seasonal hero shot. "An Emperor's Feast" leans on a quiet pattern of icons; "A Sip of Comfort" on a close, warm photograph. Different registers for different rooms.

04

Consistency

What ties them together is discipline rather than a fixed template — the same care with hierarchy, the same restraint with the property and outlet marks, the same clear placement of date, time and price. A guest reads any one of them as part of the same house.
