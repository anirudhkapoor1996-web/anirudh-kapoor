Taj West End Advertising

Brand advertising for the heritage Taj West End in Bengaluru — "Tropical Escapes in the Heart of the City" and "Morning Meetings, Powered by Nature" — built on one gold-and-image system around the property's promise: history, nature, indulgence.

Property
Taj West End · Bengaluru
Pitch
History · nature · indulgence
Pieces
Press & display advertising
Scope
Brand campaign design
Role
Art direction & layout
01Overview
Sell the stay, not the room.

The Taj West End is a heritage property — acres of garden in the middle of Bengaluru, old-world charm, a famous Rain Tree. The advertising had to sell that feeling rather than a rate: the escape, the calm, the indulgence of being somewhere that's been hosting people for over a century.

Across directions, the work holds a single gold-and-image language so every placement reads unmistakably as the same hotel.

02The Campaign
03

The Lines

A different headline for a different appetite.

"Tropical Escapes in the Heart of the City" leads with lantern-lit interiors and old-world charm for the leisure guest. "Morning Meetings, Powered by Nature" reframes the same gardens as the most pleasant boardroom in town — breakfast under the Rain Tree. Each line sells a different reason to come, off the same property.

04

The System

Holding it together is a restrained gold-and-photograph language: the Taj West End lockup in gold, a single elegant serif for the headlines, and the tagline "history, nature, indulgence" running quietly beneath. Whether the image is a glowing bar, a sunlit garden or a grid of details, the frame around it stays the same.
