About

Things.reviewed looks at products the way a designer does: materials, ergonomics, engineering, the signals the packaging sends — and then the plain question of whether it does the job.

Run by Anirudh Kapoor, a Politecnico di Milano–trained product designer. Nothing on the channel is paid for by the brands reviewed.
